About Me

Performances On
Screen & Beyond

Nusrat Faria is a Bangladeshi actress, model, television presenter, and radio jockey known for her strong screen presence and versatile performances.

She began her journey as a national-level debater, becoming a debate champion in 2010 and 2011 through Bangladesh Television programs. Transitioning into show business, she worked extensively in television commercials and radio before hosting the reality show Fair and Handsome: The Ultimate Man in 2014.

Her film debut with Aashiqui (2015), followed by Hero 420 (2016), earned her the Meril Prothom Alo Award for Best Newcomer. She later rose to prominence with films like Badsha – The Don, Premi O Premi, and Boss 2: Back to Rule, establishing herself across Dhallywood and Tollywood.
